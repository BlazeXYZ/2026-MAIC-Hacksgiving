ComForce MKE Hub

A prototype of the ComForce MKE community app for phone and desktop. One place where Milwaukee neighbors can ask for help, give help, find somewhere safe, and join the ComForce program hubs. The screens below follow a real person's path, from opening the app for the first time to a coordinator's view of the whole district. Maps use real Milwaukee streets.

Prototype · sample people and requests · try the buttons

Clear priorities

  • One urgent alert at a time, at the top of the page, only when a verified search is live nearby.
  • One main action per area. Everything else is a quieter secondary button or a link.
  • Two typefaces, each with one job. Serif italic only ever means "the AI is explaining itself."

Easy to follow

  • Home reads top to bottom: what's urgent, what you need, how you can help, then your community.
  • Laptop and phone match. The same "What do you need?" actions appear on both.
  • Every hub has the same tabs, plus its own tools, so moving between hubs feels familiar.
  • Coordinators work from one queue of decisions, each with the AI's reasoning.

Built for first-timers

  • A 3-step welcome: choose your path, set your area and hubs, and practice Quick Exit.
  • A first-visit home with a setup checklist and short explanations. The toggle in section 2 shows it.
  • Plain words: "Urgent, Today, This week" and "3 of 8 blocks searched", not codes.
  • Locked items say how to unlock them instead of hiding.

Calm and readable

  • One 8-point spacing scale: 24px inside cards, 24px between cards, 48px between page sections, 20px phone margins.
  • Few borders and boxes. Rows divide with hairlines, and cards never sit inside cards.
  • Tap targets are at least 44px, and body text is at least 15px.
